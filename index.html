Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best time for a Kadamakudy boating trip?

The absolute prime time is during sunrise (5:45 AM to 8:00 AM) or deep sunset (4:30 PM to 6:30 PM). The water behaves like a flawless mirror during these hours, temperature conditions are perfectly cool, and migratory bird activity peaks across the fish farms.

Do I need prior swimming or paddling skills for Kadamakudy kayaking tours?

Not at all. Our sit-on-top recreational kayaks are inherently stable and virtually impossible to flip over under normal usage. Combined with our high-buoyancy certified life vests and professional wilderness guides leading your trail, absolute beginners are perfectly safe.

How far is the boating location from central Ernakulam city?

Kadamakudy is highly accessible. It sits just 12 to 15 kilometers north of central Kochi hubs like Edappally or Marine Drive, translate to roughly a 25-to-35 minute peaceful road drive over the modern bridge connectors.
